How to read this guide

Orange numbered circles on the pictures show where to click, in order. Button names appear like this. Sections 5 to 9 cover everyday work for consultants, managers and finance; sections 11 to 12 are for admins.

All names, numbers and amounts in the pictures are demo data created for testing. They are not real customers or real money.

1What the system is

The KCM Travel & Tours system has two parts that work together:

  • The public website: what customers see. They browse tours, fill in the booking form or chat with Kaycee, the trip-planning assistant. Every request is saved automatically.
  • The staff dashboard (at /admin): where the KCM team handles every enquiry from first contact until the trip is finished. The main part of the dashboard is the KCM Operations Centre.

Behind both sits a private Google Sheet that stores the records, and a Google Apps Script program that enforces the rules.

The one idea to understand: the ticket

Every enquiry becomes a ticket with a number like KCM-2026-000014. The ticket follows the customer's request from start to finish: who is handling it, what the customer wants, the quotation, the payments, notes, tasks and a full history. Whether the enquiry came from the website, Kaycee, WhatsApp, a phone call or a walk-in, it ends up as a ticket in the same place.

Each ticket has three separate statuses. The system checks every action against the person's role, so hiding a menu item is never the only protection.

StatusWhat it tells you
OperationalWhere the work is in the journey, e.g. QUOTATION SENT, BOOKING CONFIRMED
PaymentWorked out from verified payments (nobody types it in), e.g. Deposit pending, Fully paid
Customer responseWhat the customer said about the quotation, e.g. Accepted, Declined

2Signing in

Staff sign-in

  1. Open the website and click Sign in at the top right (Figure 1), or go straight to /admin.
  2. Type your email address and password, then click Sign in.
  3. If your role needs it, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (see below).

First time, or forgot your password

Click Forgot or set password? under the sign-in button. Enter your email, then the 6-digit code that arrives in your inbox, then choose a password of at least 10 characters. Very common passwords (such as "password123") are refused. Setting a new password signs you out on every other device.

Two-step sign-in (authenticator app)

Super Admin, Admin, Management and Finance must use an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator. Operations staff and partners don't need one.

  1. The first time you sign in, a QR code appears (Figure 3).
  2. On your phone, open the authenticator app, tap + and scan the code.
  3. Type the 6-digit code the app shows and click Turn on two-step verification.
  4. You are shown 8 recovery codes, once only. Save them somewhere safe (a password manager, or print them). Each one works once if you lose your phone.
  5. From then on, every sign-in asks for the current 6-digit code from the app.
Tip

Lost your phone and your recovery codes? Ask a Super Admin or Admin to click Reset next to your name in Users & access. You set the app up again at your next sign-in.

The buttons at the top of every page

  • + Register enquiry: record an enquiry that came in by WhatsApp, phone, email or in person.

4How enquiries arrive from the website

Customers can reach KCM in three ways on the website. Each one creates a ticket straight away, with a ticket number shown to the customer.

1. The booking form

The customer picks a tour, a date and the number of travellers, sees an estimated price, adds their contact details, ticks the privacy (POPIA) consent box and sends it. The system works out the estimate itself, so a customer cannot change the price in their browser.

2. Kaycee, the trip-planning assistant

Kaycee asks a few simple questions (style of trip, region, dates, group size, budget) and then sends the request. She cannot confirm bookings or take payments; she only creates the enquiry for the team.

3. WhatsApp, phone, email and walk-ins

These are not imported automatically. An employee records them with + Register enquiry (see "Registering an enquiry"). The WhatsApp "Open chat" links in a ticket simply open WhatsApp; the conversation is not saved in the system unless you add a note.

5Daily work: My Workspace

My Workspace is the first page every team member sees. It shows only your work:

  • The counters: your open tickets, overdue tasks, things due this week and what you finished in the last 30 days.
  • My tickets: every ticket assigned to you, with its status, payment status and next action.
  • My tasks: change a task's status from the drop-down, or click Done to finish it.
  • Upcoming deadlines for the next 7 days, Recently completed work and My recent activity.

6Working on a ticket

Click a ticket number anywhere (it is shown in orange) to open the ticket page.

The top of the ticket

The header shows where the enquiry came from, the customer, the trip, and six key facts: operational status, payment status, customer response, priority, who it is assigned to and the next action with its due date. Click Edit under Next action to change it.

The tabs

Changing the status

  1. Click Change status.
  2. Choose the new status from the list (1).
  3. Add a note if needed (required for Cancelled, Lost and On hold).
  4. Click Change status (2).

The system checks that the earlier steps have happened. If something is missing it says so in red and nothing changes. For example, an Operations consultant cannot confirm a booking (Figure 15), and a booking can only be confirmed once finance has verified the deposit.

Adding a note or a task

Click Add note, choose the type (internal, customer communication, supplier, decision or problem) and how you spoke to the customer (WhatsApp, phone, email, SMS). You can create a follow-up task from the same note. Click Add task to create a task with a due date and priority.

7Registering an enquiry (WhatsApp, phone, email, walk-in)

  1. Click + Register enquiry at the top of any Operations page.
  2. A. Customer: name, phone, WhatsApp, email. If the phone number or email already exists, the system links the enquiry to the same customer instead of creating a duplicate. Tick the POPIA consent box once the customer agrees.
  3. B. Enquiry: where it came from (WhatsApp, phone call, email...), the type of trip and a short description.
  4. C. Travel: destination, dates, number of travellers, accommodation, transport and extras.
  5. D. Budget and payment preference.
  6. E. Next action: what happens next and by when. You can also start a draft quotation.
  7. Click Save enquiry. If you are an Operations consultant, the ticket is assigned to you automatically.

8Quotations

A quotation goes through these steps. Each step is recorded on the ticket.

StepWhoWhat happens
1. PrepareConsultantOpen the Quotations tab and click Prepare quotation. Add cost lines (accommodation, transport, activities...), the selling price or a markup, any discount, VAT treatment, deposit percentage and the valid-until date. A preview shows the total, VAT, deposit and margin; the system recalculates everything when you save.
2. SubmitConsultantClick Submit for approval. Management is notified.
3. ApproveManagementCheck the figures and click Approve. A quotation must be approved before it can be sent.

5. Customer answerConsultantClick Customer accepted or Customer declined and note how the customer replied.
Tip

Need to change a quotation that was already sent? Click New version. It becomes version 2 and the old version is kept, marked "Superseded", so you always have the history.

9Payments

Recording a payment (anyone handling the ticket)

  1. Open the ticket, go to the Payments tab and click Record payment.
  2. Choose deposit, instalment or balance; enter the amount, method (EFT, card, cash...), the bank reference and the date paid.
  3. Save. The payment shows as Unverified. It does not count as paid until finance checks it.

Verifying payments (Finance)

  1. Open Payments in the menu. The Needs verification tab lists unverified payments.
  2. Check the money is really in the bank account, then click Verify (1).
  3. Say how you checked (for example "FNB business account statement") and click Verify.
  4. If the money did not arrive, click Reject and give a reason.

Rules that protect KCM's money:

  • The person who recorded a payment cannot verify it; someone else must.
  • The same bank reference cannot be recorded twice, and pressing Save twice doesn't create two payments.
  • A verified payment cannot be changed. Mistakes are corrected with a reversal or a refund, so the history stays complete.
  • Refunds are recorded by finance and must be approved by a different finance person or an admin (Figure 26).

10For management: assigning, approving and confirming

New enquiries

Incoming Enquiries lists everything nobody has reviewed yet; Unassigned Tickets lists tickets with no owner. Use the search box and filters (status, priority, employee, source, payment status, destination, dates, overdue only) to find anything quickly. CSV downloads the list for Excel.

Assigning straight from the list (quickest)

In Unassigned Tickets, Incoming Enquiries, All Tickets and the other ticket lists, the Assigned column is a button.

  1. Click Unassigned (or the person's name, to reassign) in the row.
  2. A list of everyone who can work on tickets opens, showing how many active tickets each person already has. The current owner is marked "current".
  3. Click a name. The ticket is assigned (or reassigned) at once and the person is notified. Choose Unassign to send it back to Unassigned Tickets, or More options for a handover note, supporters and a deadline.

Moving several tickets at once

  1. Tick the box at the start of each row (or the box in the header to tick them all).
  2. In the bar that appears, choose the employee under Assign to.
  3. Click Assign. Each ticket is moved, with a handover note when it had an owner before.
Good to know

Finance staff can't own tickets, so they don't appear in these lists. Operations staff see who owns a ticket but cannot reassign it.

Assigning from inside a ticket

  1. Open the ticket and click Assign (Figure 30, marker 1).
  2. Choose the employee (2) and the priority (3). You can add supporting employees and a deadline.
  3. Click Assign (4). The employee is notified and gets a "Contact the customer" task automatically.

Handing over (leave, sick days)

Click Reassign / hand over, choose the new employee and write a short handover summary. Nothing is lost: notes, checklist ticks, quotations and history stay on the ticket, open tasks move to the new person, and the previous person no longer sees the ticket.

Confirming and completing

Only management can set BOOKING CONFIRMED, and only when a quotation has been accepted and finance has verified the deposit. A trip can be marked COMPLETED once it is confirmed; if a balance is still unpaid, management must add a note explaining why.

Operations Overview

The overview shows live figures calculated from the records: enquiries received, unassigned work, quotations, confirmed bookings, outstanding payments, overdue tasks, conversion rates, enquiries by source and destination, and each employee's workload. Choose Today, This week, This month, This year or a Custom date range.

Checklists & statuses

Under Checklists & statuses management can edit the checklist templates used for each trip type (full package, day tour, accommodation only, transfer, general), and add extra statuses if KCM needs them.

12For admins: giving and removing access

Open Users & access (Super Admin and Admin only).

Give someone access

  1. In Give someone access, type their email (1) and name.
  2. Choose the role (2): Management, Finance or Operations (only a Super Admin can create an Admin).
  3. Click Give access (3). They then sign in with Forgot or set password? to choose their password.

Change a role, remove access or reset two-step sign-in

  • Change role: pick a new role from the drop-down in the Role column (A). The change happens at once and the person must sign in again.
  • Remove access: change Status to disabled (B). They are signed out immediately and cannot sign in. Change it back to active to restore access. Their past work stays on the tickets.
  • Reset (in the Two-step column): for someone who lost their phone. They set up the authenticator again at their next sign-in.
Important

Nobody can change their own role, and nobody can be made Super Admin from the website. This stops anyone from giving themselves more power.

Activity & Audit History

Every sign-in, change, approval and payment is written to a permanent log that cannot be edited from the dashboard. Use the filter to search by person, action or ticket number.

13Partners: lodges, drivers and guides

Businesses apply to become KCM partners on the Partner with us page. They upload photos and proof of ownership and choose a password.

Reviewing an application

  1. Open Partner applications and click an application.
  2. Check the photos, the ownership documents (only staff who review partners can open these, and every view is logged) and the details.
  3. Click Approve (1) and choose a tier, Ask for more info (2) or Reject (3). For "more info" and "reject" you must write a note. The partner is emailed your decision.

Partners, trips, payouts and ratings

  • Partners: all approved partners.
  • Partner trips & payouts: record each trip a partner did, the commission and the payout; mark payouts Approved, Paid or On hold. The partner is emailed when a payout is marked Paid.
  • Ratings: guest ratings of partners, which you can hide if needed.

What partners see

Approved partners sign in on the website and see their own portal: their businesses, trips, earnings, payouts and ratings. They cannot see anything else.

14Website requests and messages

Website requests lists every booking-form and Kaycee request as it was submitted (each one also has a ticket in the Operations Centre). Messages lists notes sent from the Contact page. Both can be downloaded as CSV.

15Demo data for testing

To try everything out before going live, the system can fill itself with fake records: about 33 tickets (9 of them unassigned, so you can practise assigning) at every stage of the journey (new, assigned, quoting, waiting for deposit, confirmed, travelling, completed, lost, cancelled, on hold), customers, tasks, notes, quotations, payments waiting for verification, a refund waiting for approval, suppliers, partner applications, payouts, ratings and website messages.

Everything fake is clearly labelled: names end in (demo), emails end in @demo.kcm.test and descriptions start with [DEMO]. No emails or WhatsApp messages are sent while demo data is created.

Load it

  1. Open the Google Sheet and wait for the KCM Admin menu to appear.
  2. Click KCM Admin → Load demo data (testing only) and confirm.
  3. Wait until the message appears (it can take a few minutes). If it says "Part loaded", click the menu item again to continue.

Remove it before going live

16Going-live checklist

  1. KCM Admin → Remove demo data.
  2. KCM Admin → Remove test admin (admin@kcm.test).
  3. Give each real employee access in Users & access and make sure every Super Admin, Admin, Management and Finance user has set up their authenticator and saved their recovery codes.
  4. Turn on Google's own 2-Step Verification for the Google account that owns the Sheet: whoever controls that account controls all the data.
  5. Don't share the Google Sheet itself with staff; they use the dashboard.
  6. Check the privacy policy page and name an Information Officer (POPIA).
  7. Make a test booking on the live website and follow it all the way to Completed.

17Troubleshooting
